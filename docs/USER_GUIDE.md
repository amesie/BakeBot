# KD Pops: how to use your dashboard

**Your website:** https://bake-bot-phi.vercel.app (share this link with customers)
**Your dashboard:** https://bake-bot-phi.vercel.app/dashboard (log in with your email and password)

Forgot your password? On the login page tap **Forgot password?**, then open the email on your phone.

---

## Every day (Kaydee) · about 5 minutes after school

1. **Approve tab.** BakeBot's summary at the top tells you what is waiting.
   - Check each order, then tap **Approve** or **Decline**. To do several at once, tick them and tap **Approve ticked**.
   - Orders over 30 pops need a **quote** (your price) before you can approve.
   - After approving, tap **WhatsApp [name]: confirmation + invoice** and press **Send** in WhatsApp. BakeBot wrote the message for you.
2. **Inbox tab.** Questions BakeBot handed to you. Tap **Reply on WhatsApp**, then **Mark as dealt with**.

## Payments (Kaydee or Mom)

- **Payments tab → Waiting for payment.** When money arrives (cash, or EFT in the bank), tap **Mark paid**.
- If a customer sent a proof of payment (uploaded, or emailed to thebakebot@gmail.com with their reference as the subject), tap **View proof**. If the amount or reference is wrong, tap **Doesn't match** and BakeBot will ask them to check.
- Emails BakeBot could not match (no reference, wrong reference, no attachment) appear in **BakeBot updates** and are kept in Gmail under the label **BakeBot/Unmatched**. Check them there and mark those orders paid yourself.
- If BakeBot sent a payment reminder, a **WhatsApp reminder** button appears. Use it only if you want to nudge them yourself.
- At handover: **Ready to collect** → **Paid now** (cash) → **Collected**.

## Baking week (Kaydee)

1. **Thursday 20:00:** orders for next week close. BakeBot puts the **bake list** (how many of each flavour) on your Approve tab.
2. **Batch tab:** tap **Start baking** when you begin.
3. When pops are done, tap **Mark ready** on an order (or **All ready** for the week). Say **OK** only if they really are ready, then tap the **WhatsApp ready** button for each customer.
4. Load shedding or a delay? **Batch delayed?** → choose the new day → every customer gets the new date.
5. **Friday 14:00:** BakeBot releases **unpaid** orders for general sale and tells the customer. Paid orders are never released.

## Settings and BakeBot (Kaydee)

- **Settings:** change prices, mark a flavour sold out, set a bigger weekly cap for market week, change the collection place.
- **BakeBot answers:** BakeBot only says what you write here. Please write the **allergens** and **discounts** answers and tick **BakeBot may use this**. Use `{menu}` to show the live menu and prices.
- **Feedback:** what customers thought (taste, size, price).
- **Delete a customer's details:** if someone asks you to remove their information, enter their number in **Settings**.
- **System:** check that everything is running (all ✓). If you see ✗, tell the team.

## Golden rules

- **You decide** who gets credit, discounts or exceptions. BakeBot never does.
- Money messages come from **BakeBot (KD Pops)**, not from you personally.
- Never share your password, and log out on shared phones (**Log out** at the top).
